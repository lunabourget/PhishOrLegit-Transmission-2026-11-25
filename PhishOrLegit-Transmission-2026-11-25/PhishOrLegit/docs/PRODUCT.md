# Product definition

## Purpose

Phish or Legit? teaches first-year MyDigitalSchool students to recognize phishing, smishing, vishing, quishing, fake domains, social engineering, suspicious attachments, and requests for credentials or payments. The experience is designed for English level B1 and runs alongside a physical race board game.

The product succeeds when a player can inspect a realistic communication, identify the relevant clue, vote physically, and understand the correction without the phone taking control of the game.

## Audience and session

- Two to six players.
- Target duration: about 30 minutes, followed by a short final quiz.
- The first pawn to reach the Safe Zone wins.
- The application is 100 percent English.

## Digital experiences

### Scan me

Twenty-one of the 60 color cards contain QR codes: seven green, seven orange, and seven red. The cards open simulated emails, SMS messages, or generated voice calls. The player investigates before the physical vote. Email and SMS scenarios may award the configured Spot it bonus for identifying the exact fake detail.

### LINK

Ten LINK cards form their own pile. The player chooses to click or report a controlled link. The simulation never opens a dangerous destination. Consequences are displayed for the physical board to apply.

### Quiz and glossary

A five-question quiz measures learning after the game. A concise glossary explains the English cybersecurity terms used by the game.

## Experience principles

- Open the scanned scenario immediately.
- Preserve uncertainty until the physical vote is complete.
- Make communications credible, calm, and familiar rather than theatrical.
- Keep education in the correction, not in premature hints.
- Keep movement and victory on the physical board.
- Simulate risky actions locally and safely.
- Offer an accessible alternative to freehand annotation.

## Non-goals for the first production version

- User accounts or authentication.
- Cloud database or Supabase.
- Personal data collection.
- Digital pawns, leaderboards, currencies, experience points, or board replacement.
- Real email, SMS, payment, or calling integrations.
- Opening real phishing destinations.
